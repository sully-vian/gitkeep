A header written by hand without quotes, created in the same second as another note (hence the `-2` suffix).

YAML parsers reinterpret unquoted values: `no` and `yes` can become booleans, `2026` a number and `2026-11-16` a date.
The app should still read the title as "no", the tags as strings, and keep the date.
